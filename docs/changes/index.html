Every change vs the original Tempo

The original Tempo app is not in this repo. Each change is a mod: a feature/<name>.js file (plus optional .css) loaded into the app's renderer by tools/inject-index.mjs. No mod edits Tempo's bundle. fixture media come from headless test pages running the real mod code with fake data; real app media are prints of the real window; app capture media come from the running app. The screenshots and clips are not in this repo; their captions are kept.

Project sidebar

One sidebar organised by project replaces the left rail and the five separate route sidebars (chat, docs, design, issues, agents). Every project shows its branches, and every branch shows its decks and what is open in them.

Shortcut: ⌘⇧E (switch sidebar mode), ⌘B (collapse)

1.1Projects tree

Before: the left rail had one button per route, and each route had its own sidebar. Now: one tree lists every project, its branches (worktrees) and their chats, canvases and terminals. Clicking a row runs the app's own navigation.

How: Click a project row to fold or unfold it. Click a chat, canvas or terminal row to open it. The small + on a project starts a new worktree.

1.2Search

Before: each sidebar had its own search, or none. Now: one search box at the top filters the whole tree. A row stays when it or something inside it matches, and its parents open.

How: Click the search box and type. Backspace to clear.

1.3Issues, Agents, Docs and Organization rows

Before: Issues, Agents and Docs were route buttons in the rail. Now: they are rows at the top of the sidebar and under each project, and 'Organization' holds the ones with no project link. Data comes from read-only queries to the app's own backend.

How: Click a row to open it. Cmd+click opens a doc, issue or agent as a tab next to the chat.

Video pending: needs the real app (backend data for docs, issues and agents)

1.4Decks under branches

Before: decks (pages with their own pane layout) had no place in any sidebar. Now: each branch card lists its decks. The active deck is open and shows what is on screen; the other decks are compact rows.

How: Click a deck row to switch to it. Click its chevron on the right to open or fold its rows. The + on the branch makes a new deck.

1.5Collapsed deck rows

Now: a deck that is not open is one compact row. It shows its number and a small pill: status icons (done, running, unread), the provider of the first chat, '+N' for more, then icons for other tabs (canvas, terminal, browser, file).

How: Hover a row to see its chevron. Click the row to switch, the chevron to open it.

1.6Deck menu

Now: right-click on a deck row opens a small menu with Rename and Remove deck. Remove asks first, in place ('Its 2 tabs go back to deck 01'), with Cancel focused. The tabs of a removed deck join the active deck.

How: Right-click a deck row. Enter removes, Esc or Cancel keeps the deck. Double-click on a deck name renames it.

1.7New worktree picker

Before: the project + created a worktree with an auto name. Now: the + opens a small picker with a search field, 'New branch' (from main), the other local and remote branches, and the branches that already have a card ('switch').

How: Click the + on a project. Type to filter. Pick a branch to open it in a new worktree, or pick 'switch' to jump to its card. Esc closes.

1.8Split pane rows

Before: nothing in the sidebar knew about the split view or the dock panes. Now: the branch card lists every pane on screen (each side of the split, each dock pane) with its active tab, in screen order. Rows of one pane are linked by a thin line, and hovering a row flashes its pane in the window.

How: Hover a row to see its pane outlined. Click a row to bring that tab forward on its own side without closing the split.

1.9Drop a tab on + to open a new worktree

Now: while you drag a tab, every project + appears. Dropping the tab on one makes a new worktree for that project and moves the tab into it. The new card slides in with a short highlight, and the dock's undo plays it backwards.

How: Drag a tab from a tab bar or a dock pane over the + of a project (it turns into a 'New workspace' pill) and release.

1.10Terminal agents as agent rows

Now: a terminal that runs an agent CLI (Claude Code, Codex) is listed as an agent session, with a status, the provider logo, the task title and a 'CLI' badge, not as a plain terminal. The mod reads the tab title, or the visible terminal screen.

Video pending: needs a real terminal running an agent CLI

1.11Footer, settings and account

Now: the bottom of the sidebar hosts the plan usage line (from the usage-bar mod). Settings and the account avatar sit in the title bar next to the sidebar switch.

No video needed: static layout, no interaction

1.12Collapsed sidebar

Now: the sidebar follows the app's own 'Toggle left panel' button and Cmd+B. Collapsed, the column goes away and the workspace takes its width back; expanded, it returns.

How: Click Toggle left panel in the title bar, or press Cmd+B.

1.13Always the only sidebar

Before: a title bar switch cycled this sidebar, the native Chats sidebar and the app's own. Now: the project sidebar is the only one, with no switch. The native route sidebars stay mounted but hidden, so they keep working.

How: Nothing to do. localStorage "rtd.projectSidebar.hideNative" = "off" shows the native sidebars again.

Now: chats, projects and worktrees have Orca-style menus: Open, Open in split view or new window, Move to deck, Rename, Archive or Delete for a chat; New workspace, New worktree, Open in Finder, Editor or Terminal, Copy path for a project; new chat, canvas, doc, issue, terminal or browser, Copy branch and Pin for a worktree.

How: Right-click a chat, a project or a worktree card.

Video pending: no clip recorded yet (new feature, 2026-10-18)

1.15Worktree cards on one line

Before: each worktree card had a second line with its path and tab icons. Now: a card is one line: dot, name, the primary chip and +. A branch whose name differs shows in the tooltip.

No video needed: static layout

1.16Green status dot

The dot is green for every worktree that is ready, primary or not, and pulses while a session runs. It is gray only when the worktree is not available.

How: Hover the dot to read its state.

4.1Drop overlay with edge splits

Before: the center is one block and a tab can only be reordered or popped out into a separate window. Now: dragging a tab over a pane shows a drop overlay. The edges (25% of the pane) preview a split in that direction, and dropping there puts the tab in a new pane.

How: Drag a tab from the tab bar to the left, right, top or bottom edge of a pane, then let go.

4.2Drop on a pane's tab bar

Before: no panes, so tabs could only be reordered in one bar. Now: dropping a tab on another pane's tab bar opens a slot under the pointer and the tab joins that pane between its tabs. Dropping over the pane body adds it at the end and the pane gets a thin accent outline.

How: Drag a tab over another pane's tab bar (slot opens), or over its body (center), then drop.

4.3Nested splits

Before: at most the app's own two-panel split view. Now: splits can be nested without limit, mixing rows and columns, and every pane has its own tab bar.

How: Repeat the edge drop inside any pane, or use the split button at the right end of a pane's header.

No video needed: built by repeating the edge-split clip; the divider clip shows a nested layout

4.4Resizable dividers

Before: the split view had one fixed divider. Now: every divider between panes can be dragged, vertical and horizontal ones alike.

How: Hover a divider (it turns blue) and drag.

4.5Closing a pane, undo and redo

Before: no panes to close or undo. Now: a pane left without tabs collapses. Cmd+Z (Ctrl+Z) undoes the last layout or deck change (a split, a tab moved between panes, a pane closed, a deck created, removed, renamed or reordered), several presses undo several, and Cmd+Shift+Z (Ctrl+Shift+Z, Ctrl+Y) redoes. Each workspace has its own history. The shortcut only acts when nothing else owns it (typing, the canvas, a webview).

How: Close the last tab of a pane, then press Cmd+Z. Cmd+Shift+Z goes forward again.

4.6Decks: several arrangements per workspace

Before: one set of tabs per workspace. Now: each workspace keeps several named decks. A deck is a whole arrangement (the layout, which tab sits in which pane, the tabs the center shows and its active chat). Switching hides the other decks' tabs (they are not closed). A tab belongs to one deck only, a new deck starts with a new chat, a deck left without tabs goes away (except the workspace's last one), and decks are saved and come back on reload. Decks have numbered names (Deck 01, Deck 02...). Other mods use window.__RE_TEMPO_DECKS__.

How: Create a deck from the sidebar's deck cards and click a card to switch. The clip uses a small stand-in bar for those cards.

4.7A + button in every pane

Before: only the center had a + menu. Now: each pane has its own + right after its last tab, with the same items (New Chat, Canvas, Doc, Issue, Terminal, Browser, and Open for existing canvases, docs, issues and files). The app's own action runs and the new tab lands in that pane. The Open in split view button sits at the right end of the header.

How: Click the + in a pane's header and pick an item.

4.8Pane header look

Before: the split view had its own plain headers. Now: every pane header looks like the center's: same tab shape, active tab highlight, close buttons, + and split buttons.

No video needed: static styling, visible in every pane clip

4.9Several canvases at once

Before: one canvas on screen at a time. Now: any number of canvases can be open in different panes, each with its own view, selection and undo. Opening a canvas that is already on screen gives the new pane its own second copy instead of moving the first one. The chat's canvas selection follows the last clicked canvas.

How: Use a pane's + > Open and pick a canvas, or Open in split view on a canvas tab.

4.10Drop a chat on + to start a new workspace

Before: no way to branch a chat into a workspace by dragging. Now: dropping a chat tab on a + (a pane's or the center's) turns the + into a New workspace pill, the tab flies into it, a new workspace is created with a copy of that chat and a toast offers Undo.

How: Drag a chat tab over a + and drop it there.

4.11Layout is saved and restored

Before: nothing to save. Now: the panes, their sizes and tabs are saved per workspace (localStorage) and come back when the window reloads.

4.12Panes only on the chat route

The dock works only on the chat route with a workspace open. On other routes the panes are hidden and the app looks normal; they return when you come back to chat.

No video needed: static switch with no motion

4.13Solo mode

Before: no way to focus one pane. Now: one pane, or the center, fills the whole area for a while. The other panes are hidden, not closed, and Exit solo brings the layout back as it was.

How: Press Cmd+Shift+Enter, or click the solo button in a pane's header. Esc or Exit solo leaves.

Video pending: no clip recorded yet (new feature, 2026-10-18)

4.14Placeholder for a tab in an external window

Before: a tab torn out into a popout window simply left its pane. Now: its spot shows Open in external window with Bring back and Focus window, and the tab returns to the same pane and position when that window closes.

How: Click Bring back to return the tab, or Focus window to jump to it.

Video pending: no clip recorded yet (new feature, 2026-10-18)

4.15Canvas popovers stay in their pane

Before: a canvas in a pane drew its comment composer, menus and tooltips relative to the whole window. Now: they stay inside that pane.

How: Open a canvas in a pane and comment or open a menu: it opens in that pane.

Video pending: no clip recorded yet (new feature, 2026-10-18)

4.16Empty panes and decks

A pane without tabs collapses and its neighbours take the room. The center hides when none of its tabs is left. A deck is never empty: a new deck starts with a new chat and a deck left without tabs goes away.

How: Close the last tab of a pane or deck.

No video needed: the close-and-undo clip shows a pane collapsing

4.17Deck API: Close all and Move tabs

Other mods can now close every tab of a deck (closeAll) or move tabs to another deck (moveTabs) through window.__RE_TEMPO_DECKS__, with undo. The project sidebar uses it for its deck menu.

How: In the sidebar, open a deck's menu and pick Move tabs to or Close all tabs.

Before: changing the app means rebuilding and restarting. Now: the watcher talks to the app through its debug port, hot-swaps the stylesheet on a .css save (the page keeps its state) and reloads the window on a .js save.

No video needed: background tool; it needs the real app's debug port
